# AI Services Marketing Catalog

This catalog presents the AI service portfolio in business-friendly language for demos, sales conversations, solution overviews, and customer-facing documentation.

## Portfolio Snapshot

The repository includes a broad clinical AI suite that supports the full care journey:

- intake and documentation automation
- OCR and data structuring
- clinical summarization and timeline generation
- recommendations and guideline validation
- nursing workflow prioritization
- discharge planning and discharge quality assurance
- sepsis risk detection and ICU support
- conversational access to hospital data

## Documentation and Data Capture

### AI Auto-Population Service
Transforms clinician free text into structured medical data while preserving traceability, validating fields, and respecting role-based access rules. Ideal for accelerating documentation, reducing manual data entry, and improving structured data quality.

### OCR Parsing Service
Converts image-based medical documents into machine-readable text and structured JSON. Ideal for bringing scanned forms, handwritten notes, and uploaded clinical documents into digital workflows.

### Clinical Summary Service
Turns structured patient data into concise, professional clinical summaries that preserve medical terminology, abbreviations, and detail. Ideal for fast documentation, handoffs, and standardized summary generation.

### Patient Timeline Service
Builds a chronological timeline of clinically important events from structured and semi-structured patient data. Ideal for giving clinicians a rapid, traceable view of the patient journey across encounters, labs, procedures, medications, and notes.

### Discharge Report Generation API
Generates comprehensive discharge summaries from clinical documentation using AI. Ideal for organizations that want to shorten discharge documentation time while keeping physician review in the loop.

### Discharge QA Service
Validates discharge reports against patient records, onsite documentation, templates, and quality rules. Ideal for improving discharge report completeness, consistency, safety, and compliance before final release.

## Clinical Decision Support

### Clinical Recommendations Service
Generates evidence-based, actionable clinical recommendations from patient context, including medication, diagnostic, treatment, monitoring, lifestyle, and referral guidance. Ideal for surfacing next-step suggestions in a structured, integratable format.

### Lab Result Interpreter Service
Interprets lab values in context, highlighting abnormal findings, patterns, trends, and follow-up considerations. Ideal for helping care teams quickly understand the clinical significance of complex or changing lab panels.

### Medical Guideline Validation API
Checks active medical orders against evidence-based guidelines and flags contraindications, missing protocol steps, and patient-specific risks. Ideal for strengthening clinical governance and reducing variation in care.

### Medication Test Orders Validation Service
Validates medications and diagnostic test orders against patient data to identify interactions, contraindications, dosing concerns, and sequencing issues. Ideal for improving order safety before execution.

### Sepsis Early Detection
Analyzes patient time-series data to detect early signs of sepsis risk and produce structured risk assessments with scoring and organ dysfunction reasoning. Ideal for proactive deterioration monitoring and early escalation workflows.

## Care Coordination and Operations

### Nurse Task Prioritization Service
Ranks nursing tasks by urgency and clinical importance using patient monitoring data, medication timing, pending tasks, and abnormal findings. Ideal for helping nursing teams focus attention where it matters most during busy shifts.

### Smart Discharge Planner Service
Assesses discharge readiness using clinical and operational inputs, then identifies blockers, follow-up needs, medication reconciliation concerns, and planning summaries. Ideal for coordinating safe, efficient, and timely discharges.

### ICU Summarizer API
Generates ICU daily summaries and handoff presentation decks from structured flowsheet data. Ideal for critical care teams that need faster handoffs, clearer communication, and consistent daily review materials.

## Conversational Access and Digital Front Door

### MedAI Assistant
Provides a chatbot-style interface for clinical and operational workflows, including chat orchestration, SQL generation, validation, formatting, and patient detail retrieval. Ideal for teams that want natural-language access to hospital information and guided AI interactions.

## Positioning Summary

This portfolio can be positioned as a modular clinical AI platform that helps healthcare organizations:

- reduce documentation burden
- improve care coordination
- support safer clinical decisions
- strengthen quality assurance
- speed up discharge and handoff workflows
- unlock structured insights from unstructured medical data

Each service can be deployed independently or combined into larger workflow solutions depending on the customer's use case.
